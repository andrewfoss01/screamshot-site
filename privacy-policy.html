1. Scope and short summary

This policy covers the Screamshot Windows application. Screamshot processes captures on your device. It does not automatically send screenshots, recordings, project contents or clipboard images to Andrew Foss. It has no advertising, analytics, telemetry or automatic crash-report upload feature. The Microsoft Store edition uses Microsoft's services for purchase and license verification. Visiting our website or sending a support email involves separate services, described below.

2. Information processed on your device

When you capture or import media, Screamshot processes the selected screen pixels or media file, annotations, calibration, timing, edits and export settings. Captures may contain personal or confidential information visible on your screen. Default filenames can include the name of the application being captured and the capture date and time.

If enabled, video recording captures microphone audio, system audio, webcam images, the cursor, click indicators or keystroke indicators. Microphone, system-audio and webcam capture are optional. The webcam can preview before recording when switched on. Keystroke and click indicators monitor input during active recording and draw transient badges into the recorded picture. Those indicators can expose passwords or other sensitive input on screen; turn them off when recording sensitive activity. Screamshot does not create a separate background keystroke log.

Capture shortcuts normally use Windows' standard hotkey registration. If you assign a shortcut that Windows reserves for itself, such as Win+Shift+S (which many laptops send from an Fn "snip" key), Screamshot installs a Windows keyboard hook while it runs so it can respond to that shortcut. The hook checks each key press only to recognise the assigned shortcut, hides that shortcut from Windows, and lets every other key pass through unchanged. It does not record, store or transmit what you type. Removing the shortcut in Screamshot's shortcut setup turns the hook off.

Local preferences include save locations, filename patterns, capture hotkeys, per-tool annotation styles, symbol favorites and recently used symbols, recording options and selected device identifiers. The symbol library works offline. Project files contain original media and editable annotation data, not just the flattened export. Other people can recover that original media if you share an editable project, including content covered by an annotation.

3. Clipboard, storage and retention

Still screenshots are copied to the current Windows clipboard on capture and after annotation changes. Updates replace the current image. When Windows clipboard history is enabled, Screamshot permits still images in history and uses local history timestamps and pixel comparisons to identify its own images. It removes the previous verified image for the same editing project after the replacement is available. It does not clear unrelated history or turn history on. Windows history size limits may exclude large images, while the full image remains on the normal clipboard. Screamshot marks copies to prevent cloud clipboard synchronization. Object-only and GIF copies are excluded from Windows history. Other applications with clipboard access, including third-party clipboard managers, may still read or retain those contents. Copying a selected annotation also transfers its editable geometry, style and any embedded image for pasting into a project; on still images the complete screenshot is included too. GIFs are copied when you request it.

Exports and saved projects remain in the locations you choose. Preferences are stored in your Windows user profile. Working media, extracted project media, GIF clipboard files and unfinished recording segments can be stored in the Windows temporary directory under Screamshot. Successful recording completion normally removes its raw segments, but final preview media and other working files can remain. When Screamshot starts, it deletes working files from earlier sessions that are more than three days old. Failed or interrupted recordings are kept for recovery until recovered. There is no guarantee that all temporary data is automatically erased at exit or uninstall.

Small diagnostic logs are stored locally under the Screamshot user-profile folder. They record application version, process/run identifiers, startup and shutdown reasons, periodic activity timestamps, hotkey registration counts and technical exception details. Error details can contain file paths or values involved in an error. Capture pixels, clipboard images, annotation contents, keystrokes and command-line file paths are not deliberately collected for these logs. The logs are not uploaded automatically. Each edition retains its current log and up to three rotated logs, normally about 1 MB total, plus a small session-status file. You can delete these logs when Screamshot is closed.

You control how long your saved captures and projects remain. Uninstalling never deletes captures and projects you saved. Uninstalling the standalone edition keeps its settings and logs; uninstalling the Microsoft Store edition lets Windows remove that edition's settings and logs. To remove local data, close Screamshot, delete captures and projects you no longer need, clear clipboard/history as appropriate, and remove its settings and temporary folders. Deleting temporary files also removes associated recovery data. Secure deletion is not guaranteed by ordinary file deletion.

4. Microsoft Store and other recipients

In the Store edition, Windows and Microsoft Store check your license, trial expiration and purchase entitlement. They may use your Microsoft account, device and transaction information under Microsoft's policies. Microsoft handles payment; Screamshot does not ask for or store payment-card details. Windows may cache license information for offline use. The Store edition also asks Microsoft Store about twice a day whether a Screamshot update is available; Microsoft Store downloads and installs updates when you choose to. Andrew Foss may receive sales or usage reports that Microsoft provides to publishers; these do not include your screenshot or recording contents from Screamshot. Microsoft's privacy statement is at https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement .

Screamshot has no automatic upload or sharing destination. Information can leave your device when you choose to paste, email, upload, print or otherwise share a capture using another application or service. Saving to a synchronized folder may also cause its provider to upload files. Those providers' policies apply to their services.

5. Support and website visits

If you email andrewfoss01@gmail.com, you choose what to send. We receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to respond, investigate your request and maintain relevant support records. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive recordings. Redact examples before sending them. Email and website hosting providers process information needed to deliver those services. Support information may be disclosed if required by applicable law. This app policy does not describe website cookies, hosting logs or other website-specific collection that has not been reviewed.

We keep support information as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant records and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information held by Andrew Foss, or to raise a privacy concern. We will respond subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements. We cannot delete local captures we have never received or data controlled by Microsoft and other providers.

6. Your controls and security

Choose what to capture and whether to enable audio, webcam or input indicators. Pause or stop recording when appropriate. Revoke camera/microphone permissions using Windows settings. Use suitable Windows accounts, permissions, device encryption and access controls for sensitive files. Screamshot does not itself encrypt saved media or project files; Windows or your storage provider may offer encryption. We do not promise that recordings or clipboard contents are inaccessible to other software running with sufficient permissions on your device.

7. Other people and policy changes

Obtain any permission or consent required to record other people, their voices, screens or information, including information about children. This application is a general-purpose capture utility and does not require a Screamshot account or collect users' ages. We will revise this policy when app data practices change. Updated notices will be made available in the application and on the published policy page; material new collection or sharing will be explained before it is introduced, with consent where required.
